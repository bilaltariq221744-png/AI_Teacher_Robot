They are called pleasant sounds. There are some other sounds which are harsh and irritant. We do not like to hear such sounds. They are called unpleasant sounds. Such sounds are called noise. The examples of these sounds are the dragging of a chair on the floor, the pressure horns of wagons and buses, etc. They produce noise pollution.
