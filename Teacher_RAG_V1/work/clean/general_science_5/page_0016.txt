Chapter 01 Classification of Living Organisms
Biodiversity As we have read in previous class, the number of kinds of living things present at a particular place is called biodiversity. The type of plants and animals no longer found in this world are called extinct e.g. dinosaurs. Many organisms that are very likely to become extinct in near future are called endangered organisms such as Bengal tiger, Rhinoceros, etc. There are many endangered animals in Pakistan e.g. snow leopard, green turtle, hawk, Indus dolphin and markhor. Take a chart paper. Cut pictures of various plants and animals from old newspapers or magazines and paste on the chart paper. You have placed all the organisms together at one place. What is it?
