Safety Measures > © © OVOO Keep your area clean. Remove the unnecessary objects around you. Workplace should not be wet to avoid slipping. Be careful while using sharp tools. Put them at their proper place after use. Be careful while working with fire. You should not wear polyester or nylon clothes as they catch fire instantly. Avoid electricity operated tools and devices. If necessary, seek help of the teacher. Do not use spit for folding or turning pages. Use a piece of foam soaked in clean water.
