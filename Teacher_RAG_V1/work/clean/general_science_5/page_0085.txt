Open and Closed Circuits You have learnt that on turning the switch ON, the circuit becomes complete or CLOSED due to which the current starts flowing through it. On turning the switch OFF, the circuit becomes incomplete or OPEN, so the current stops flowing through the circuit. It is called open circuit.
