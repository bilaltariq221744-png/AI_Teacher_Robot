We can see across transparent objects.
Faint image is seen across a translucent object.
Shadow is made behind opaque objects as light cannot pass through them.
Light travels in a straight line.

Sound is produced by a vibrating body.
Sound can pass through air, water and solid objects.
The speed of sound in air is less than in liquids and solids.
The intensity of sound decreases with increase of distance from its source.
Harsh and irritant sounds are unpleasant sounds. Such sounds are called noise.
Noise is harmful for human health. Therefore reducing noise is necessary.
