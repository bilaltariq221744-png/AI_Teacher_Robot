Classification and Characteristics of Flowering Plants
Take the seeds of wheat, rice, maize, pea, bean and gram. Find out in which seed there is
one cotyledon and in which seed there are two cotyledons. Write your observations.
Monocot Plants
The flowering plants whose seeds have one cotyledon are called monocot plants.
Sugarcane, wheat, rice, bamboo and maize are the examples of monocot plants.
