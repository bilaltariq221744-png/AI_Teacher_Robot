Structure of a Flower
A flower is a very important and attractive part of the plant. In some plants there is only one
flower at a stalk e.g. rose. Some flowers are produced in bunches e.g. mustard. The stalk of
the flower is called pedicel. The flower has four parts at the pedicel. These occur in the form
of four whorls. The four parts of flower are sepals, petals, stamens and carpel.
