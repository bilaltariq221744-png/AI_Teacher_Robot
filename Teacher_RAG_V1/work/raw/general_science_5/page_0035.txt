Flowers and Seeds
In asexual reproduction only one plant produces new plants. Flowers do not take part in
this type of reproduction. The other parts of the plant e.g. root, stem and leaves give rise to
new plant. The plants produced from these parts have a great similarity with the parent
plant. Stem cutting, layering, bulb and tuber, etc. are the ways of asexual reproduction.
i. Layering
Often some branches of shrubs become buried in the soil. It
is called layer. A layer produces new roots. When we cut and
separate it from the plant, it develops into a new plant. This
process is called layering. Layering is also done artificially.
Breeding of lemon, jasmine and lychee is done by layering.
