When we look up at the sky on a clear night, we see countless tiny lights commonly known
as Stars. But scientifically, they are not all stars. Infact they include galaxies, stars, planets,
Moon and some other objects. All these objects are called heavenly bodies. These heavenly
bodies are a part of the vast space around us which is collectively known as the universe.
Different countries have sent artificial satellites and other bodies in space for research and
surveillance purpose.
Space Exploration
After seeing the heavenly bodies one becomes curious to know the realities of the Earth
and space. The invention of a rocket made it possible to travel beyond our
atmosphere into the vast space. We also could know how our Earth and solar system came
into existence. The space age began on October 4, 1957 when Soviet Union launched
Sputnik-1 in the orbit of the Earth.
