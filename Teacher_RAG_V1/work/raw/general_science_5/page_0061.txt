Chapter 05 Physical and Chemical Changes of Matter
Difference between Physical and Chemical Changes
We have already read that there are two types of changes: Physical changes and Chemical
changes. If during a change of any material, if no new material is formed with different
properties, then such change is called a physical change. Physical change can be reversed,
such as on heating, the solid wax becomes liquid but remains a wax. On cooling the liquid
wax becomes solid but remains a wax.
A change in which a new material (with different properties) is formed, is called chemical
change. The burning of paper is a chemical change as ash is formed in the process. The
chemical change cannot be reversed.
