Chapter 08 Structure of the Earth
The core is the central part of the Earth. It is the hottest and heaviest part of the
Earth.
Oceans, glaciers, rivers, lakes, springs and underground water are sources of
water.
There are three types of soil; sand, clay and silt.
Clay is useful to make pottery, sand for glass and silt is good for cultivation.
The decayed matter is called humus. It makes the soil fertile.
