Structure of Seed
The outer covering of seed is called seed coat. It protects the tiny embryo which is present
inside seed. There is a pore on seed coat. It is called micropyle. Embryo consists of
cotyledons, which store food. The axis of embryo is between the two cotyledons. The end
of axis towards the pointed end of seed is called radicle. When seed germinates, its radicle
forms roots. The other end of the axis is called plumule. It gives rise to the shoot.
