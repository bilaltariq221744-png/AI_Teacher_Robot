Electromagnet
Cana magnet be made with the help of an electric current. Let us perform an experiment.
Wind an insulated copper wire very closely
over an iron nail or a rod so that it becomes a
long coil.
Attach the connecting wires to the two
ends of the coil and connect them to
battery through a switch as shown in the
figure.
Keep the switch OFF and bring some steel
pins near one end of the nail. Does the nail
attract them?
Turn the switch ON and see if the nail attracts the
pins now.
What inference do you draw from it?
Predict what will happen if the current is switched OFF.
Turn the switch OFF to verify your prediction.
What do you conclude from this experiment?
When an iron nail or a rod is placed in a current carrying
coil, it becomes a magnet. This is known as electromagnet.
Electromagnet is a temporary magnet. It does not remain a magnet when the current is stopped in
the coil.
