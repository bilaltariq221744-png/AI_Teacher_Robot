Stirring
Activity 5.4
. Take two beakers or glasses. Mark them as A and B.
. Add aspoon of sugar (solute) in both the beakers.
. Then pour a cup of water (solvent) in both beakers.
. Stir sugar in beaker A with a glass rod. Do not stir
sugar in beaker B.
_ What have you observed? Dissolving sugar in water
In the above activity, the dissolving of sugar in such a manner is called stirring. When you
stir sugar in beaker A it dissolved faster, whereas the
sugar in beaker B dissolved slowly. If the sugar is not stirred even then it will dissolve but it
will take more time.
