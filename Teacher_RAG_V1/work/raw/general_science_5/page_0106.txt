Interesting Information
Clay is useful to make pottery, sand for glass and silt is good for cultivation.
There are three parts of the Earth on the basis of its structure.
The outer layer is known as crust. It consists of sea and solid rocks.
The middle layer is mantle. It consists of extremely hot thick fluid.
