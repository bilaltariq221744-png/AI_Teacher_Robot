Information reports are mostly written in the present tense. This is because the information
presented on the topic will generally be based on factual texts.
Depending on the topic of the text, vocabulary specific to the subject will normally be used. For
example, if the text is providing information on buildings, it will likely utilize related words
and phrases such as, 'site', 'location', 'architect', 'design' etc.
It is important for students to realize that they should use general nouns when writing on
their topic. The information included in their text should be information that is true generally
and this should be reflected in the use of the generic noun classifying it, for example: The
school buildings are funded by the government.
Information reports are an example of formal non-fiction writing. Here, the passive voice is
used to keep the attention firmly on the object. It has to draw the attention away from the
doer or speaker.
