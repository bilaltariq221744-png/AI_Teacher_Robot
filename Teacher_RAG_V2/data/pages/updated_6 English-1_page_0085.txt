FASCINATING FACTS ABOUT THE MARKHOR
(NATIONAL ANIMAL OF PAKISTAN)
Over the last 8 years of running Green
Global Travel, there's been an unfortunate
amount of bad news to report for nature and
wildlife. But the comeback of the markhor– the
National Animal of Pakistan– from the brink of
extinction has been one of the world's great (but
littleknown)conservationsuccessstories.
When we first wrote about this
impressively horned wild goat in 2011, the
population in its native range (which includes
mountainous regions of Afghanistan, India, and
Pakistan) had shrunk to around 2,500
individuals.
